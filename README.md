# Deeper and Darker — Conquer the Sculk Dimension

Deeper and Darker 9minecraft Mod expands the scary sculk caves into a massive adventure. It adds a brand-new dimension called the Otherside that you can reach through the Ancient City. Instead of running away from the deep caves, you now have a reason to stay and fight. This mod fills the darkness with new monsters, glowing blocks, and powerful armor sets. You can finally explore the deepest parts of the world and find amazing treasures.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Pedro270707**, **ang-xd**, **nitrodynamite18**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
